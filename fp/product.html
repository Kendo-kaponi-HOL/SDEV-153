PrismArc GPU

The PrismArc GPU is a next‑generation graphics processor engineered for creators, gamers, and AI developers. It combines cutting‑edge architecture, adaptive cooling, and intelligent rendering to deliver exceptional performance across gaming, design, and computation workloads. Built for speed, precision, and visual fidelity — PrismArc redefines what a graphics card can do.

Why PrismArc?

PrismArc isn’t just another GPU — it’s a creative powerhouse. Designed with advanced AI‑assisted rendering, ultra‑efficient power delivery, and a sleek RGB‑infused chassis, it’s the perfect balance of performance and style. Whether you’re building cinematic worlds or training neural networks, PrismArc gives you the edge.

Core Features

  • Adaptive Arc Cooling System: Triple‑fan vapor chamber design with dynamic RPM control for ultra‑quiet operation.
  • RGB Spectrum Sync: Customizable lighting across the shroud and backplate with 16.8 million color options.
  • NeuralFrame Rendering Engine: AI‑assisted frame generation for up to 40 % higher FPS in supported titles.
  • Creator Mode Optimization: Enhanced color accuracy and rendering pipelines for 3D modeling and video editing.
  • QuantumLink Architecture: PCIe 5.0 interface with 24 GB/s bandwidth for lightning‑fast data transfer.
  • SilentEdge Power Regulation: Smart voltage balancing ensures stable performance while minimizing heat output.
